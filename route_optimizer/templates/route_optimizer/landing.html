Health & Environmental Impact

Understanding Dhaka's air crisis

🌫️

PM2.5 Micro-Particles

Particles under 2.5µm penetrate deep into lungs and the bloodstream. Our engine avoids heavy diesel corridors to reduce long-term cardiovascular exposure.

💨

PM10 Road Dust

Coarse dust from construction sites and heavy vehicle friction blankets major arteries. The detour algorithm identifies lower-traffic bypass routes.

🏭

NO₂ & Vehicle Emissions

Nitrogen Dioxide from brick kilns and idling traffic accumulates at congestion bottlenecks. Route optimization steers clear of dense emission clusters.

Algorithm Workflow

How eco-routing works

Step 01

Live API Ingestion

Queries real-time WAQI monitoring stations across Dhaka to map active pollutant concentrations at route checkpoints.

Step 02

Detour Optimization

Calculates mid-point bearing angles and lateral waypoints to route around high-pollution hotspots while minimising added distance.

Step 03

Parallel Dispatch

Evaluates all 8 priority strategies concurrently and presents a side-by-side comparison of distance, time, and AQI scores.
